# FootfallIQ Attendance

The public product story for WhatsApp-based employee attendance, distinct from FootfallIQ's event-pass offering.

## Language

**Service operator**: Spopsy (Sole Proprietorship), the business operating FootfallIQ Attendance.

**Employee**: A person assigned to a site and shift who explicitly shares their current location to mark attendance.

**Site**: A work location with a configured attendance radius.
_Avoid_: Branch, geofence (when referring to the location itself)

**Shift**: A scheduled working period assigned to an employee at a site.

**Attendance event**: A check-in or checkout submitted for a shift.

**Location verification**: A comparison of submitted coordinates with the assigned site's configured radius. It does not establish the employee's identity.

**Attendance exception**: An attendance event that needs review, such as an expected check-in that has not been received.

**Supervisor**: The person responsible for following up on attendance exceptions at assigned sites.

**Business owner**: A person who needs attendance visibility across sites and shifts.

**Demonstration scene**: A clearly illustrative moment in the employee or supervisor attendance journey.

**Approved template**: Exact WhatsApp message wording approved by Meta for use with the product. Product-owner acceptance of wording and illustrative sample interactions do not by themselves establish Meta approval.

**Draft template**: Supplied WhatsApp message wording awaiting Meta approval. It is distinct from both an approved template and an invented sample interaction.
